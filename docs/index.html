Scope, source limitations & import guidance

Choose Import CAB / logs to open a CAB archive or readable Windows logs, event JSON/XML, Entra sign-in JSON/CSV exports, or existing Windows Diagnostics JSON reports. Supported CAB members are analyzed together without selecting files individually. Coverage lists every archive entry and explains skipped, partial or failed analysis; Unsupported entries, including unparsed binary logs, are not treated as analyzed. Archive member counts do not use the 20 selected-file limit. Raw logs are combined into one investigation; report exports retain their supplied findings. UTF-8 and UTF-16 text are supported. Files stay in this browser session and are not uploaded or saved by the site. Local timestamps without a timezone are excluded from the UTC chart. Redaction is best-effort; review the exported brief before sharing. Missing findings do not establish endpoint health.
